Repaint the first image, a grey blockout of a level of our mobile game, as a finished painted scene, in exactly the
style of the second and third images, which are two levels of the same game: the same soft painted isometric
look seen from above at an angle, the same light, the same hazy glowing sea and sky with no horizon line anywhere, the
same white marble slabs with chamfered, broken edges laid on chunky dark rock, the same dark rocks at the bottom
corners before the eye.

Keep the layout of the blockout exactly, every part where it is and as large as it is; only paint it:
- At the bottom, a low ledge of marble in the water where a small woman in white and terracotta stands.
- Above it, a terrace of marble on dark brown rock, with a sandy marble block on it, and a stair at the right that
  climbs from the far end of the ledge to the terrace.
- In the middle of the picture, above the head of that stair, the glowing jagged outline in the blockout: a tall crack
  in a sheet of glass, about twice as tall as the woman, glowing warm white. It is a crack in glass, not lightning:
  nothing above it in the sky.
- In the upper half, the pale grey part of the blockout: the same place again, as seen in a mirror. It stands upright
  on the same still sea, exactly as a room is seen in a mirror on its wall: never upside down, never floating in the
  sky. Its slabs, rock, stair and block are where the blockout has them, painted silver, pale and cool, a little
  translucent, like a memory. On it, a ring of soft white light and, behind it, a gate whose opening is white.
- Where the blockout has a white cloud at the left of that pale part, the mirror's image is missing: soft white fog
  there, nothing under it.

The idea: the goddess's mirror has grown into a vast sheet of glass standing across the whole scene at the crack,
facing us, in a perfectly still sea; what lies beyond it is only its reflection. The glass itself is almost invisible:
a few long soft vertical gleams where the light catches it, fading into the water at its foot and into the sky above;
no line where it stands in the water. The woman alone has no reflection.
The time: a silver dawn in mist, pale silver-blue and soft lilac, a soft low sun glowing behind the haze at the upper
right, a few motes of light drifting up from the crack.
No statue of a goddess, no colossus and no statues anywhere in the background or the sky: only the mirror,
the place, its reflection, the still sea and the haze.
No text, no frame.
