The first image is the blockout of a level of our game, drawn through the game's own eye (seen from above at an angle, isometric): six stone floors of ONE great tower rising out of a still sea, Theron the sculptor's tower in ancient Greece, built like the Tower of the Winds in Athens. The second image is a concept painting of an earlier version of the same tower: take its painting style, its ancient Greek architecture, its golden morning light, its still sea, mist and colours.

Paint the tower as true ANCIENT GREEK classical architecture in the manner of the Tower of the Winds of Athens:
- Keep the six floors exactly where they are, each with its own layout of slabs, stairs, the round white ring and its light on each floor, the small veiled statues, the woman in white with terracotta bands.
- The round bronze discs set in the floors, each with an arrow, are the winds' discs: paint them as round bronze plaques set flush in the marble, each with a carved wind's face blowing and an arrow.
- Under each floor's cornice a carved frieze of the winds as winged flying figures, as on the Tower of the Winds; between the floors colonnades of fluted Doric columns under a straight entablature; walls of large squared marble blocks; under the lowest floor a stepped base going down into the water.
- On the very top a small conical roof of marble with a bronze Triton weather vane turning in the wind.
- The wind is felt: the statues' veils streaming one way, wisps of mist and a few leaves blown across the tower in long soft streaks.
- Post and lintel ONLY: NOT ONE arch, no vaults, no arched or round-headed windows or doors. Nothing medieval, Roman or fantasy.
- The same angle as the blockout (from above, isometric), the whole tower in the picture, nothing cut off. No text, no letters, no numbers.
