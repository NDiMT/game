- A Greek key (meander) band along each floor's edge; faint ancient paint on the frieze only (faded terracotta red and deep blue).
- The hoists as ancient Greek builders' cranes: timber frames lashed together, hemp ropes, bronze pulleys, stone platforms hanging in them.
