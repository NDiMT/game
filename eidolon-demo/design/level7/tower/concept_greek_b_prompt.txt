This is a concept painting of a tower in our game. Make it truly ANCIENT GREEK by changing only its architecture, keeping everything else exactly as it is (the composition, the floors and their slabs, the stairs, the white rings and their light, the small veiled statues, the woman in white, the hoists, the sea, the light, the colours, the style):
- Remove EVERY arch: the arched openings at the tower's foot and every arched or round-headed window and door become open colonnades of fluted Doric columns under straight stone lintels, or rectangular openings under straight lintels. Not one curved opening anywhere.
- Every floor stands on a Doric colonnade with a full Doric entablature (architrave, a frieze of triglyphs and metopes, a cornice), as on the Parthenon; the tower's foot a stepped temple base (crepidoma) going down into the water.
- The walls of large squared marble blocks in even courses; no rough medieval masonry, no buttresses.
- No text, no letters, no numbers.
