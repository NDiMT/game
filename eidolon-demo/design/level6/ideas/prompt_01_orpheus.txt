Her soul follows her. The heroine walks along a winding path of slabs toward the gate. One step behind her, in her very footsteps, follows a faint translucent woman of pale blue-white light, veiled, with no face under the veil, glowing softly: the soul she is leading out of the ruin. The heroine must never turn back to look at her. The path loops once around a broken column so it can turn without going back. Late dusk, melancholic, the first stars.
