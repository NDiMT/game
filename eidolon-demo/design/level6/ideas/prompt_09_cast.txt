The hall of casts. A ruined hall of white plaster casts on an island: plaster casts of statues, hands and faces stand about on the slabs. The heroine kneels at the edge and presses a glowing square of wet white plaster onto the sea: where she presses, a square patch of solid marble floor with a small column on it rises from the water, an exact copy of a patch elsewhere on the island, whose outline glows faintly.
