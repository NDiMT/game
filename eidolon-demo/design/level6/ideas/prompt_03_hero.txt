The temple doors of Hero of Alexandria. On a terrace stands a temple gate with two bronze doors. Before it a marble altar with a small fire burning on it and thin smoke. From the altar a bronze pipe runs down to a large bronze bucket hanging on a chain over a wooden pulley; the bucket is filling with water and sinking, and as it sinks the two doors swing open. On the other end of the chain a heavy stone counterweight slab rises dripping out of the sea and becomes the missing step of the stair that leads up to the gate.
