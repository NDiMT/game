The statue that follows. A heavy veiled marble statue of a woman, taller than the heroine, standing on a square base, glides over the slabs after her. Narrow channels of still water separate the islets. The heroine stands safe across a channel; the statue has stopped at the water's edge facing her, unable to reach her, a little foam at its base. One cracked loose slab lies in the path between them, half sunk.
