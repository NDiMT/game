The greedy cup. A huge round marble cistern like a giant cup stands on the island, filled with water to the very brim; a bronze statue of a man stands in its middle up to his chest. Beside it the heroine turns a bronze crank. From a hidden siphon spout low on the cup's side a thick waterfall gushes down into a lower stone basin, where a wooden raft floats up level with a quay. The overflow is the answer.
