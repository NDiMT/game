THE LEVEL:

The bronze giant. A giant bronze man, three times the heroine's height, strides along a stone path that rings a small island. At a fork of the path lies a round pivoting stone slab that decides which way he goes. On one branch a jagged dark rock spike juts up from the path. Shallow channels carved into the white slabs lead from the spike downhill to a rectangular stone mould beside a gap in the causeway, and a little glowing molten bronze runs along one channel. The heroine stands near the fork.
