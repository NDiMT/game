THE LEVEL:

False fires. A golden hazy dusk over several small rocky islets, each with a stone beacon tower topped with a bronze fire basket; two beacons burn with warm fire, the others are dark. A pilotless ancient ship with a pale square sail drifts toward a burning beacon on a rocky reef, and the timbers of an earlier wreck float near the reef in a line of stepping stones toward the gate's islet. On a cliff a white marble statue stands watching the sea. The heroine stands by an unlit beacon holding a torch.
