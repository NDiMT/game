THE LEVEL:

The wound cart. A small bronze cart on four wheels stands on a grooved stone track that runs along a causeway over the water, a marble cube riding on it. At the start of the track a tall marble cylinder holds a lead weight on a cord, and the cord is wound around the cart's axle over a row of small bronze pegs. The heroine is winding the cord. Across the water the track ends on a square stone plate in front of the gate's islet.
