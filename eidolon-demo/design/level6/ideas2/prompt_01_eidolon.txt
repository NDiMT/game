THE LEVEL:

Her image. On a small islet of slabs the heroine stands half hidden behind a broken column. A few slabs away, on a narrow causeway, stands a glowing translucent copy of her, the same slender figure made of soft white light, like a phantom. A tall hooded statue of dark smoky violet glass with pale cracks stands in the shallow water at the middle of the island, staring at the glowing copy; a thin red line of light runs from its eyes and ends exactly at the copy. From the other side a heavy veiled white marble statue of a woman on a square base glides toward the glowing copy, and between them lies one cracked slab.
