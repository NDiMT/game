THE LEVEL:

The owl and the birds. On a round marble islet a bronze owl sits on a short pillar, turned toward a small bronze tree full of little bronze birds with open beaks. Beside the pillar a sealed bronze jar is filled by a thin stream of water from a carved channel, and a curved bronze siphon pipe leads from the jar to a bronze pail hanging on a chain from the owl's pillar. Next to the islet a row of square stepping stones has risen just out of the still water. A marble cube stands on the slab between the owl and the bronze tree, blocking the owl's view of the birds. The heroine is about to step onto the first stepping stone.
