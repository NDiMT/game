# Level 6: ten ideas with chains of machines (24/11)

The owner, after The Faithful: "find another ten, completely original, research well, with multi-mechanism steps and
cleverness". Four research passes were run in parallel: puzzle games whose mechanisms chain (Blue Prince, Cocoon, A
Monster's Expedition, Monument Valley, the GO games, Animal Well, Baba Is You, Railbound, Öoo and others), ancient and
medieval machines (Hero's *Pneumatics* and *Automata*, Philo, Ctesibius, Archimedes, the Antikythera mechanism, theatre
machines, al-Jazari), classic multi-step mechanical puzzles (sequential discovery, puzzle boxes, Chinese rings, Hanoi,
weighing and pouring, Rube Goldberg and domino logic, escape-room dependency charts), and Greek myths that hide a
mechanism. Their shared lesson: the state of one machine (a water level, a body on a slab, a sound) becomes the input
of the next, one thing does several jobs, a threat becomes a tool, the puzzle first looks like a contradiction, and
nothing in it is idle.

Each idea chains three to five mechanisms, painted in the game's style by the image model (`gen.py`, with the level 2
and level 5 concepts as references; `prompt_base.txt` and `prompt_<n>.txt`), shown to the owner as a page:
https://claude.ai/artifact/2yP7BimHGKAoAJtbtgcx2Z (`page.html`, its pictures here at 720x1280).
