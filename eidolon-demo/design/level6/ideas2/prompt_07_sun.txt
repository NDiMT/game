THE LEVEL:

Light and shade. Beside a square sunken pool stands a big polished bronze sphere on a marble stand. The heroine stands between the low sun and the sphere so that her long shadow falls across it, and the pool beside it has drained, showing square stepping stones on its wet floor. Across the island a second bronze sphere stands in full sunlight and its pool is brimming, a small wooden raft floating high in it. A marble cube stands near the first sphere.
