THE LEVEL:

The ringing cauldrons. Large bronze cauldrons on tall bronze tripods stand close together along the paths of a terraced islet. The heroine has just struck the first one with a bronze mallet, and faint circular ripples of sound spread through the misty air around the cauldrons one after another. Beside each ringing cauldron a square stepping stone has risen out of the still water. On a far islet near the gate, a giant with many closed eyes all over his body lies asleep beside the last cauldron, which is filled to the brim with water.
