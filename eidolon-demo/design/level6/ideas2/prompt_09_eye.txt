THE LEVEL:

The shared eye. Three hooded grey stone statues of old women stand in a triangle on an islet, all eyeless; one holds up a single glowing eye in her open palm, about to pass it to the next. In front of the one holding the eye, a path of translucent ghostly slabs over the water is solid and lit where her gaze falls, and fades into faint glassy outlines beyond it. The heroine crouches behind the sisters.
