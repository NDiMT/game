THE LEVEL:

The hound and the fox. Two bronze animals, a lean hunting hound and a fox, run across the slabs of a small island, the hound chasing the fox onto a narrow pier that ends above a gap in the causeway. At the end of the pier the hound has just reached the fox, and both are turning to grey stone from their paws upward, frozen in mid-leap. The heroine watches from a slab nearby.
