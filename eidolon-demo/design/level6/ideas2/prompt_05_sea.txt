THE LEVEL:

The sea gives back. Half sunk in the still sea stands the colossal stone head of a bearded old god, his mouth open wide at the water's level like a dark cave, and the heroine is pushing a marble cube into it. On another islet near the gate, a round stone spring basin bubbles, and a fluted column drum is rising out of it, shoving a veiled marble statue off the spring's edge into the water. A bronze gong hangs on a wooden frame beside the heroine.
