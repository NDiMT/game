The ruin's columns, toppled into bridges. Swipe into a standing column from beside it and it falls away from you over
as many places as it is tall (the number on it): over the still water it lies as a bridge, walked only along its
length, onto it from its foot or from beyond its top, never from the side. A column whose top lands beside another
standing one knocks that one on the same way (dominoes). Stepping on the winch raises every column back onto its base
out of the water (the first level's winch; nothing is ever lost for good).

The board (tools/go/topple_trial.py LEVEL6; board.png, the moves drawn by board_anim.py): the first push at the start
knocks two columns into one bridge; from its end the tall column is pushed north, a bridge to the gate's islet, but one
cannot step off a bridge sideways onto it; back at the start the second pair is pushed east, and now its chain stops at
the tall column's empty foot: the way to it. Pushed first, that pair knocks the tall column east into the sea (the
trap; the winch sets it right). 27 places walked (about ten swipes, the long ways at one swipe), one way only, no dead
end in 201 states, every column needed.

Its line, to settle with the owner: "Her soul stayed in the ruin, / without a face." (the script's ninth truth).
