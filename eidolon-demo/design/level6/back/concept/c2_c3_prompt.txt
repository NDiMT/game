Paint a new, finished concept of this level of our mobile game, in exactly the style of the second, third and fourth images, which are three levels of the same game: the same soft painted look seen from above at an angle, the same hazy glowing sea and sky with no horizon line anywhere, the same thick white marble slabs with chamfered, broken edges laid on chunky faceted dark rock.
