The first picture is a crop of a frame of a calm, painterly puzzle game at dusk: a soft hazy sky glowing peach and rose, a perfectly still sea that mirrors it, and, at the lower right, part of an island of white marble slabs on dark rock. Out in the hazy sea stand ten plain white statues of veiled women on small square bases, roughly modelled, with their reflections, seen from high above as the game's camera sees everything.

Repaint only those ten statues, in the style of the second picture's veiled statues: tall marble women draped in veils from head to foot, still and silent, soft and pale, half lost in the dusk haze, painted softly and luminously, low in contrast, in the haze's own peach, rose and lilac. Keep every statue exactly where it is, at exactly its size and pose, and seen from exactly the same high angle as now: we look down on them from above, we see the tops of their veiled heads and shoulders, and they stand in the still water. Never paint them from the front at eye level. Their square bases sunk in the water, so they rise straight out of the sea; each with its soft reflection under it, as now. The ones further up the picture are further away: fainter and more given to the haze.

Do not change anything else: the sky, the haze, the sea and the island stay exactly as they are; nothing is added and nothing is taken away. No text.
