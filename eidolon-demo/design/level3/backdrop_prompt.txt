A background painting for a calm mobile puzzle game, portrait 9:16, soft and dreamy like concept art.
The whole picture is one soft gradient: a pale blue sky at the top fades into a wide band of bright white haze at about one third of the height, and below it a calm pastel lilac and blue sea fades softly toward the bottom. There is no horizon line: the sky, the haze and the sea flow into each other in one piece.
On the left, far off, a colossal statue of Aphrodite, the Venus de Milo: armless, her hair gathered in a knot, her head turned a little to her left. She is low-poly, built of large flat triangular facets, in pale lilac-white marble, lit softly from the left. She is very big: her head is about 8% from the top of the picture, her body fills the left third, and her hips and everything below them dissolve into the bright haze.
Much farther away, behind her, a few small strange rock formations stand in the haze: a rock leaning far over, a natural arch, a cluster of thin needles. They are faint, muted lilac-blue silhouettes, almost swallowed by the haze, rising no higher than her waist. She stands in front of them.
The middle and the right of the picture stay calm and empty. No islands, no birds, no buildings, no other statues, no text.
