This is a frame of a game at dawn: an island of dark rock with white marble stairs and a gate, a great veiled marble statue standing in the calm pink and lilac sea at the left, and a woman on a small white slab with a winch in front. Under the water between her slab and the foot of the stairs lie pale sunken stepping stones.

Add only this, and make it beautiful: water lilies all around the island, floating on the calm water close around its rocks and slabs, around the veiled statue's feet and around her slab: round lily pads of many sizes in loose clusters and drifts, some overlapping, a few with their edges curled up, in colours that belong to this dawn (a muted dusky green-grey touched with rose and lilac where the light falls on them), and here and there a water lily in flower, pale pink and white with a soft golden heart, catching the low sun, and a few closed buds standing a little out of the water. The pads lie flat on the water in the picture's own perspective, with soft reflections and gentle ripples about them, in the same soft painterly style and light as the picture.

Keep the water clear over the pale sunken stones between her slab and the stairs. Leave open water in the middle distance and a band of open water along the bottom edge of the picture.

Keep everything else exactly as it is: the island, its rocks, slabs, stairs and gate, the statue, the woman, the winch, the sky, the sun, the far rocks and the text.
