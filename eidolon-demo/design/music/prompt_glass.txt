Create a 2 minute 30 second piece of original ambient instrumental music for a calm puzzle game set on small islands of marble and rock in a still pink dawn sea. It plays quietly in the background while the player thinks, so it must be sparse, airy and peaceful, with a gentle mystery.

Style: a minimal, atmospheric contemporary drama score. Slow evolving pads, a soft low drone, very sparse felt piano notes and harp or lyre notes that ring out with long reverb, high violins held almost silently, occasional soft swells of low strings. Calm, contemplative, a little sad, like light on still water. Very slow, free tempo, in a modal minor with an ancient Greek colour.

The female voice: a solo female voice singing a few long notes without words (only "ah", no lyrics) appears only twice, very far away and soft; most of the piece has no voice.

No drums, no percussion beats, no guitar, no electronic beat, no choir singing words. One continuous, gently flowing piece with no sudden changes, ending softly so it can loop.

[0:40 - 1:05] A soft swell of low strings; the distant female voice, a few long notes.
