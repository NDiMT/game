Using the same ancient Greek vase-painting style as the reference image (black figure and red-figure painting, terracotta orange ground, black glaze, fine incised lines, a little red and white), paint a single round tondo, like the painted inside of an ancient Greek drinking cup (kylix), seen straight on, filling the whole square image edge to edge.
Inside the circle: the head and shoulders of a young woman in strict profile, facing right, eyes calm, her hair bound up with a band, a long neck; behind her a small dove. A thin meander border runs round the inside of the circle. Outside the circle the square corners are plain black glaze.
Strong, simple, clear silhouettes with big shapes, so the picture still reads if it is cut into four quarters. No text, no letters, no signature, no frame other than the round border. Flat colours, no photographic shading.
