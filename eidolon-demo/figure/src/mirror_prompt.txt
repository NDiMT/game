Using the same ancient Greek vase-painting style as the reference image (black figure and red-figure painting, terracotta orange ground, black glaze, fine incised lines, a little red and white), paint a single round tondo, like the painted inside of an ancient Greek drinking cup (kylix), seen straight on, filling the whole square image edge to edge.
Inside the circle: a young woman in a long white chiton and a red mantle standing in the middle, seen from the side, holding up a round hand mirror and looking into it; a dove at her feet; behind her two thin columns. A thin meander border runs round the inside of the circle. Outside the circle the square corners are plain black glaze.
Strong, simple, clear silhouettes with big shapes, spread over the whole circle, so the picture still reads if it is cut into a three by three grid. No text, no letters, no signature. Flat colours, no photographic shading.
