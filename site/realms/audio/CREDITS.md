
## Sound effects samples (audio/sfx/)

The 22 short one-shot notes in `audio/sfx/*.mp3` (celesta, orchestral harp, french horn, brass section,
timpani, taiko drum, choir aahs, tubular bells, reverse cymbal, glockenspiel) are taken unmodified from the
**FluidR3_GM** General MIDI soundfont by Frank Wen, as rendered to MP3 by Benjamin Gleitzman's
[midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) (`FluidR3_GM/<instrument>-mp3.js`, base64 decoded).
FluidR3_GM is released under the **MIT License** (Copyright (c) 2000-2002, 2008, 2012 Frank Wen).
All other sound effects are synthesized live in `sfx.js`.
