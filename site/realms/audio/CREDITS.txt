ORBIS: Five Crowns - orchestral samples
=======================================

The instrument samples in this folder (*.bin) come from the FluidR3_GM
General MIDI SoundFont by Frank Wen (fluid@pdq.net), released under the
MIT License. They were taken from the per-note MP3 renderings repackaged
by Benjamin Gleitzman in https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts
(FluidR3_GM, gh-pages branch), also MIT licensed.

Only a subset of notes is kept (every 3rd or 4th semitone; music.js
pitch-shifts to the neighbouring notes). Each .bin file packs the
original, unmodified MP3 notes of one instrument:

  uint32 LE  header length H
  H bytes    UTF-8 JSON {"notes": [[midi, offset, length], ...]}
  ...        MP3 data; note i = bytes [4 + H + offset, 4 + H + offset + length)

File          FluidR3_GM instrument
strings.bin   string_ensemble_1   cello.bin    cello
horn.bin      french_horn         brass.bin    brass_section
harp.bin      orchestral_harp     choir.bin    choir_aahs
flute.bin     flute               oboe.bin     oboe
celesta.bin   celesta             glock.bin    glockenspiel
bells.bin     tubular_bells       timpani.bin  timpani
taiko.bin     taiko_drum          pizz.bin     pizzicato_strings
