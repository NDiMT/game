The attached picture is the FIRST FRAME of the video.
Bring this ancient Greek black-figure vase painting to life as flat 2D cut-out animation, as if the painted figures on
the vase began to move. LOCKED-OFF STATIC CAMERA: no zoom, no pan, no tilt, no camera movement at all. The orange clay
ground, the black bands at the left and right, and everything that is not named below stay perfectly still.
The drawing stays exactly as painted: the same flat colours, the same outlines and incised lines, no shading, no 3D,
no light effects, no new objects, no new figures, no text. Every figure keeps its exact look, clothes and proportions
from the first frame to the last. There is very little movement: ONE simple action, calm, slow and steady. Whatever
is not named below does not move at all. In the last two seconds everything has come to rest and holds perfectly
still. Single continuous shot, no cuts. Silent: no music, no voices.

WHAT HAPPENS:
- The boat lies still on the calm water; it does not travel and rocks only very slightly.
- The veiled woman kneeling in it slowly lowers her white hand the last small distance until her fingertips touch
  the water. Thin pale rings spread slowly outward from the touch. She holds still.
- There is no oar and she does not row. Her body hardly moves; only the arm goes down.
- The red veil covers her head completely the whole time; no face is ever seen, on her or on her image.
- The image of the boat and of the woman in the dark water, upside down, follows her small movement exactly; its arm
  is white like hers, and the two hands meet at the surface.
- The sea is a flat, calm, dark band and stays so. The sun does not move.
