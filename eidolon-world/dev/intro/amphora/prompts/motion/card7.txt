The attached picture is the FIRST FRAME of the video.
Bring this ancient Greek black-figure vase painting to life as flat 2D cut-out animation, as if the painted figures on
the vase began to move. LOCKED-OFF STATIC CAMERA: no zoom, no pan, no tilt, no camera movement at all. The orange clay
ground, the black bands at the left and right, and everything that is not named below stay perfectly still.
The drawing stays exactly as painted: the same flat colours, the same outlines and incised lines, no shading, no 3D,
no light effects, no new objects, no new figures, no text. Every figure keeps its exact look, clothes and proportions
from the first frame to the last. Movement is calm, slow and continuous, one action flowing into the next. In the
last second everything comes to rest and holds still. Single continuous shot, no cuts. Silent: no music, no voices.

WHAT HAPPENS, IN ORDER:
- The empty boat rocks very gently on the still water and drifts a little; its oar swings slowly.
- The red veil floating on the water drifts slowly away to the right, undulating; the thin pale rings round it widen
  and fade.
- The small man on the shore at the left walks three slow steps forward to the edge of the shore, stretches his arm
  out toward the sea, holds it there, then lets the arm sink and bows his head.
- The reflection of the boat trembles a little as water does.
- The sea is a flat, calm, dark band and stays so. Nothing rises out of the water. The sun does not move.
