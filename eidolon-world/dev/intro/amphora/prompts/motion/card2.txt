Bring this ancient Greek black-figure vase painting to life as flat 2D cut-out animation, as if the painted figures on
the vase began to move. LOCKED-OFF STATIC CAMERA: no zoom, no pan, no tilt, no camera movement at all. The orange clay
ground, the black bands at the left and right, and everything that is not named below stay perfectly still.
The drawing stays exactly as painted: the same flat colours, the same outlines and incised lines, no shading, no 3D,
no light effects, no new objects, no new figures, no text. There is very little movement: ONE simple action, calm,
slow and steady. Single continuous shot, no cuts, no fades, no dissolves. Silent: no music, no voices.

WHAT HAPPENS:
- The young woman in the red dress stands on the low black block (first picture). She steps down off the block to the
  right, one foot and then the other, walks two small plain steps, and comes to stand on the ground exactly where she
  is in the last picture. She arrives there about two seconds before the end and then stands perfectly still.
- She walks naturally, upright, IN STRICT PROFILE FACING RIGHT the whole time; she never turns toward the viewer and
  makes no gestures. Her feet stay bare.
- HER FACE STAYS A BLANK, SMOOTH, EMPTY WHITE SHAPE THE WHOLE TIME: no eyes, no eyebrows, no nose, no mouth.
- The black block does not move, slide or change. The kneeling man, the goddess with her mirror, the dove and the
  temple do not move at all.
