The attached picture is the FIRST FRAME of the video.
Bring this ancient Greek black-figure vase painting to life as flat 2D cut-out animation, as if the painted figures on
the vase began to move. LOCKED-OFF STATIC CAMERA: no zoom, no pan, no tilt, no camera movement at all. The orange clay
ground, the black bands at the left and right, and everything that is not named below stay perfectly still.
The drawing stays exactly as painted: the same flat colours, the same outlines and incised lines, no shading, no 3D,
no light effects, no new objects, no new figures, no text. Every figure keeps its exact look, clothes and proportions
from the first frame to the last. Movement is calm, slow and steady. In the last two seconds everything has come to
rest and holds perfectly still. Single continuous shot, no cuts. Silent: no music, no voices.

WHAT HAPPENS (two things, at the same time):
- The woman on the rock walks slowly forward, to the left: three or four small, slow, plain steps along the top of
  the rock and down its gentle left slope, and then she stops and stands still. She stays upright, in strict profile
  facing left the whole time, with her hand at her cheek; she never turns toward the viewer. Her hair, her light
  shawl and her skirt move gently in the wind behind her. Her face stays exactly as painted.
- The small man in the doorway at the right lowers his hand, turns and goes inside; then the door closes: a plain
  door fills the doorway and stays shut. He does not come out again.
- The rock, the wave scrolls, the sun, the ground lines and the door frame do not move at all.
