The two attached pictures are the FIRST FRAME and the LAST FRAME of the video.
This is an ancient Greek black-figure vase painting, animated as flat 2D painting. LOCKED-OFF STATIC CAMERA: no zoom,
no pan, no tilt. The orange clay ground, the black bands at the left and right, and everything not named below stay
perfectly still. The drawing stays exactly as painted: flat colours, the same outlines and incised lines, no shading,
no 3D, no glow, no sparkles, no light rays, no new objects, no text. Single continuous shot, no cuts. Silent.

WHAT HAPPENS:
- In the middle a WHITE MARBLE STATUE of a woman stands on a low black block (first picture). She comes alive: colour
  flows slowly into the marble, like paint soaking into cloth, rising steadily from her feet up to the top of her
  head. Her dress turns purple-red with its black hem, her skin warms to living cream-white, and her long hair
  darkens to black, until she is exactly the woman of the last picture.
- She does NOT move while this happens: the same pose, the same place, on the block. Only her colours change.
- Her face stays a BLANK, SMOOTH, EMPTY WHITE SHAPE the whole time: no eyes, no eyebrows, no nose, no mouth.
- The change is finished about one second before the end, and then everything is still.
- The kneeling man, the goddess with her mirror, the dove, the temple and the block do not move or change at all.
