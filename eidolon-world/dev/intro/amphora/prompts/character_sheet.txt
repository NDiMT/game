A character model sheet, painted flat in authentic Attic black-figure vase-painting technique on plain warm orange
terracotta clay (flat black slip silhouettes in strict profile, thin incised detail lines, added cream-white, added
matt purple-red; no shading, no gradients, evenly lit, no vase, no border, no text, no labels). Five full-length
standing figures in one row on a thin ground line, all the same scale, well separated, all facing right:

1. THERON, the sculptor: a lean man painted in black slip. Short curly black hair bound with a thin purple-red
   fillet, a pointed black beard. He wears a short belted tunic that ends at mid-thigh, black, with a broad
   purple-red sash hanging from his left shoulder to his hip. Bare legs, barefoot.
2. THERON again, the very same man in the very same tunic and sash, kneeling on one knee with both hands raised.
3. IONE AS A MARBLE STATUE: a slender young woman standing stiffly, entirely in added cream-white: skin, hair and
   dress all the same white, like carved marble. Her hair is gathered in a low knot with a fillet. She wears a
   long sleeveless ankle-length dress with fine vertical incised pleats. Her face is smooth and blank: no eye, no
   mouth. Barefoot.
4. IONE ALIVE: the same young woman, same height and build and the same dress shape, but now in colour: skin in
   added white, long wavy BLACK hair worn loose down her back and bound with a thin purple-red fillet, the dress
   in matt purple-red with fine incised pleats and a narrow black border at the hem. Her profile face has one
   almond eye. Barefoot.
5. IONE VEILED: exactly figure 4, same dress, but a long purple-red veil is drawn over her head and face and hangs
   to her waist; only her white arms and feet show.

Wide image; generous empty clay round the figures.
