A character model sheet for a storyboard, painted flat in authentic Attic black-figure vase-painting technique of
about 530 BC, in the manner of Exekias: confident flat black slip silhouettes in strict profile, rich fine incised
detail lines (hair curls, beard, muscles, cloth pleats, patterned borders), added cream-white, added matt purple-red.
Plain warm orange terracotta clay ground. No shading, no gradients, evenly lit, perfectly flat. No vase, no border,
no text, no labels, no numbers.

Six full-length figures in one row standing on one thin ground line, all drawn at the same scale, tall (they fill
most of the height of the image), well separated, all facing right:

1. THERON, the sculptor: a lean, strong man of about forty painted in black slip. Short tightly curled black hair
   bound with a thin purple-red fillet; a pointed black beard; one almond eye incised. He wears a short belted
   tunic ending at mid-thigh, black with fine incised pleats, and a broad purple-red sash that hangs from his left
   shoulder to his hip. Bare legs, barefoot. He stands upright holding a small mallet in his lowered right hand.
2. THERON again, the very same man with the very same hair, beard, tunic and sash, kneeling on one knee with
   both open hands raised in prayer.
3. IONE AS A MARBLE STATUE: a slender young woman standing stiffly upright on a low rectangular plinth block,
   arms at her sides. EVERYTHING about her is added cream-white like carved marble: skin, hair and dress alike,
   with fine incised lines. Long wavy hair hanging loose down her back to her waist, bound with a thin fillet.
   A long sleeveless ankle-length dress belted at the waist with fine vertical pleats. HER FACE IS BLANK: the front
   of her head is a smooth empty white oval: no eye, no eyebrow, no nose line, no mouth, nothing drawn inside it.
4. IONE JUST COME ALIVE: exactly the same woman as figure 3, same height, same build, same long wavy hair to the
   waist, same dress shape, but now in colour: skin in added white, the hair BLACK with a thin purple-red fillet,
   the dress matt PURPLE-RED with fine incised pleats and a narrow black border at the hem. Barefoot, taking a
   small step. HER FACE IS STILL BLANK: a smooth empty white oval: no eye, no eyebrow, no nose line, no mouth.
5. IONE WITH HER FACE: exactly figure 4 in every respect (same black wavy hair to the waist with the purple-red
   fillet, same purple-red dress with the black hem border, white skin), but now her profile face is drawn: one
   large almond eye, a fine eyebrow, a straight nose and a small mouth.
6. IONE VEILED: exactly figure 5 in the same dress, but a long purple-red veil is drawn over her head and face and
   hangs down to her waist, hiding her hair and face completely; only her white forearms and white feet show.

Wide image. Plain clay to all four edges.
